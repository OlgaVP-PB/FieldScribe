# FieldScribe v2.5.0 - Less typing, less scrolling

Improvements driven by tester feedback from Pawel Olszewski (Leif Andersson's
herring / Clupea group, biobanking at Studsvik). The theme of his feedback was
clear: in the field, hands are busy and messy, so every bit of typing and
scrolling removed is a win.

## Added

**Type-to-filter tissue picker.** The Organism part list has 73 controlled ERGA
terms, and finding one meant a lot of scrolling. Each tube now has a search box:
type "liver", "gill", "gonad", and the list filters instantly. Only the official
controlled terms stay selectable, so COPO validation is unaffected, and anything
you have already ticked stays visible while you filter.

**GPS fills the location.** When you capture GPS, the app now reverse-geocodes
the coordinates and prefills the Collection location (country / region / place),
so you no longer retype what GPS already knows. It is online-only and
best-effort, stays silent when offline, and never overwrites something you typed.

**Import / preload samples.** A new panel on the first page imports a sample list
from **CSV or Excel**, and offers a blank template to download. Each row becomes
part of a specimen record; putting the same Specimen ID on several rows gives one
specimen several tubes. This is ideal for repeat sampling (e.g. the monthly
Studsvik set): prepare the list once, import it, and in the field just complete
and export. Organism part and purpose are auto-uppercased and underscored to
match the controlled terms.

## Changed

**Wider-screen tube layout.** On laptop and tablet (720px and wider), tube fields
now lay out in two columns instead of a single tall stack, cutting the scrolling
when a specimen has many tubes.

## Not done (and why)

A few of Pawel's ideas are deliberately parked:

- **Linked accounts across devices** would need a university back end and GDPR
  review; the workaround stays Back up / Restore to move data between devices.
- **Photos attached to specimens** and **linking DNA/genotyping results** belong
  to the broader "general documentation / lab" direction, not the field-metadata
  core, and are being scoped separately.
- **Free-text / custom tissue terms** cannot be added, because an unlisted term
  fails ENA/COPO validation - the type-to-filter picker is the answer instead.
