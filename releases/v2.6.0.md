# FieldScribe v2.6.0 - Dictation (free text)

A first step on voice input: you can now **speak into the free-text fields**
instead of typing them.

## Added

A microphone button appears next to the specimen path's free-text fields -
**Collected by, Collection location, Habitat, and Collection method**. Tap it and
speak; the words are typed into the field. Tap again to stop.

This uses the **browser's own built-in speech recognition**. Two honest points,
both surfaced in the app:

- It **sends the audio to the browser maker** (e.g. Google, for Chrome) to
  transcribe, and it **needs a connection**. A short note says so the first time
  you use it.
- It works where the browser supports it (Android / Chrome is best). Where it is
  not supported - notably **iOS Safari** - the button simply does not appear.

For dictation that is **fully on-device and works offline** (no audio leaving the
phone), the separate **Vosk voice extension** in
[FieldScribe-extensions](https://github.com/OlgaVP-PB/FieldScribe-extensions) is
the route; it is heavier and still in prototype.

## Not yet

This is free-text dictation only. **Voice-driven structured entry** (speaking
values that fill the controlled fields - tissue, purpose, IDs - via a constrained
grammar) is the bigger, more powerful direction and is scoped for later.
