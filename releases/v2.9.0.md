# FieldScribe v2.9.0 - three more BCDM fields in the field

Following the v2.8.0 BOLD5 / BCDM export, Ben Price (NHMUK) reviewed the BCDM
columns that FieldScribe was leaving blank and flagged three worth capturing at
collection time. All three are now in the specimen form, **optional**.

## Added

- **Subspecies** - in the taxonomy section, next to the other rank fields.
  Maps to BCDM `SUBSPECIES`.
- **Identified by** - the name of the person who identified the specimen, also
  in the taxonomy section. Maps to BCDM `IDENTIFIED_BY`.
- **Site code** - a short code for the sampling location, in the collection
  section below the location field. Maps to BCDM `SITE_CODE`.

All three are optional and leave the field blank in the export when empty, so
nothing changes for anyone who doesn't use them. They are saved, re-loaded on
edit, and flow into the BOLD5 / BCDM `Submissions` export like every other field.

## Still exported blank (for reference)

The remaining BCDM columns with no field in FieldScribe yet: museum ID,
collection code, funding source, subfamily, tribe, identification method,
taxonomy notes, reproduction, short note, notes, voucher type, specimen linkout,
associated taxa, associated specimens, collection end date, elevation/depth
accuracy, collection notes, and collection event ID.
