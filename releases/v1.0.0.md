# FieldScribe v1.0.0

**Field Data Collection for Planetary Biology** — first public release.

A mobile-friendly, offline-capable web app for capturing biological and
environmental sample metadata in the field, then exporting it as CSV.

## Highlights
- **Works anywhere, offline.** Pure HTML/CSS/JS, no backend, no login. Data
  stays on the device; export to CSV when back online.
- **Two sample categories.** Organism/specimen and environmental sample, each
  with a tailored field set.
- **Built for field conditions.** Large touch targets, GPS capture with
  accuracy, auto-fill of collector name and "use last location" for repeated
  sampling.

## What you can record
- **Core (all samples):** sample ID/barcode, collector, date, GPS (lat/long +
  accuracy), altitude/depth, habitat, temperature, pH, field notes.
- **Organism:** kingdom, scientific & common name, NCBI TaxID, sex, life stage,
  organism part (kingdom-dependent vocabularies).
- **Environmental:** sample type (water/soil/sediment/air filter/Malaise
  trap/…), collection method, volume/mass, salinity, dissolved oxygen,
  turbidity.

## Export
CSV compatible with Excel, R, Python, and database import.

## Known limitations (addressed in the v2.0.0 line)
- CSV column names are FieldScribe's own, not yet aligned to the ENA / ERGA
  manifest schema.
- One flat form per sample; no specimen→tube splitting and no lab/vouchering
  stages.
