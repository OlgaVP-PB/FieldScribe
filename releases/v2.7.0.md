# FieldScribe v2.7.0 - Remembered collector identity

The first concrete step of the field-entry redesign (the goal: minimise what has
to be typed in the field).

## Added

Your **collector identity** - name, affiliation, ORCID, and voucher institution -
is now **remembered on the device** and pre-filled into every new specimen
session. Enter it once; after that it's already there. It saves whenever you save
a specimen, and you can always overtype it for a one-off.

Stored only on the device (like all FieldScribe data); specimen path for now.

## Context

This is one of the agreed "cheap, certain wins" from the field-entry design work
(see the design notes). Bigger pieces - the high-throughput bag-and-tag flow,
barcode-or-type ID capture, and any role for voice - are being scoped with actual
field samplers before building, since the real workflow decisions (one species vs
mixed catch, pre-labelled vs bulk, etc.) are theirs to make.
