Metadata standards & credits

Specimen sampling follows the ERGA Sample Manifest (European Reference Genome Atlas — Astrid Böhne and colleagues), which builds on the Darwin Tree of Life (DToL) sample manifest (Mara Lawniczak and colleagues) and the iBOL / BOLD (Barcode of Life) template. Population genomics uses the BGE-ERGA Population Genomics manifest (José Melo-Ferreira and colleagues); barcoding exports to the BOLD / iBOL specimen template.

eDNA / environmental follows the ENA sample checklists implementing the GSC MIxS environmental packages (Genomic Standards Consortium), with environmental context from the ENVO ontology. Exported as an ENA-ready TSV.

FieldScribe is an original contribution of the Planetary Biology Strategic Area at SciLifeLab, with gratitude to these communities whose open standards it builds on.
